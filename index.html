Common questions.

常見問題。

What material are the cards made from? 卡片用甚麼材質？

316L stainless steel — the same grade used in watches and surgical tools. Finished with brushed satin in gold, silver, or gunmetal tones.

316L 不鏽鋼 — 與腕錶及醫療器械同級。以拉絲工藝處理，提供金色、銀色及槍色選擇。

Will the engraving wear off? 雕刻會磨掉嗎？

No. The text is laser-etched 0.02mm into the surface — it's permanent. The card will age, but the words won't disappear.

不會。文字以激光蝕刻 0.02mm 深度 — 是永久的。卡片會隨時間有自然痕跡，但文字不會消失。

What size is the card? 卡片有多大？

85.6 × 54mm — exactly the same as a credit card. Fits in any wallet or cardholder.

85.6 × 54mm — 跟信用卡一模一樣。放進任何銀包或卡套。

How do I care for my card? 怎樣保養？

Wipe with a soft cloth. Avoid prolonged contact with water or chemicals. The brushed finish develops a natural patina over time — that's part of its character.

用軟布擦拭即可。避免長時間接觸水或化學品。拉絲表面會隨時間產生自然光澤變化 — 這是它的個性。

How long does shipping take? 送貨要幾耐？

Hong Kong: 2-4 business days. Taiwan: 5-7 business days. Macau: 3-5 business days.

香港：2-4 個工作天。台灣：5-7 個工作天。澳門：3-5 個工作天。

Can I request a custom quote? 可以刻自己的句子嗎？

Not yet. Every quote in our collection is carefully curated for meaning, length, and visual balance on the card. Custom engraving is coming soon.

暫時未有。每句金句都經過精心挑選，確保意義、長度和卡面視覺平衡。自訂刻字即將推出。

Do you ship to Taiwan and Macau? 有冇寄台灣同澳門？

Yes. Flat rate: HK$35 to Taiwan, HK$40 to Macau.

有。台灣均一運費 HK$35，澳門 HK$40。

I haven't seen one in person. What does it feel like? 未見過實物，手感如何？

It has the weight of a good pen — about 30g. Cool stainless steel with a brushed satin texture you can feel under your fingernail. Credit-card sized, 0.8mm thick — sits flush against your other cards without adding bulk.

大約 30g，跟一支好筆差不多重。拉絲不鏽鋼表面，用指甲能感受到紋理。信用卡大小，0.8mm 厚 — 放在其他卡旁邊，不會增加厚度。
